Deliverable 3 — Strategic Rationale

Why UGC Talking Head?

At $200K/week budget on Reels, the thumb-stop rate in the first 3 seconds is the single most important performance driver.

A genuine testimonial outperforms a polished, on-brand video that checks every box but feels like advertising.

The talking head format builds trust and feels native in the feed — exactly what a 30–55 female audience needs, as they filter out anything that looks like marketing.

👤 Authenticity > Production

A real woman telling her real story. No forced script, no studio, no effects.

📱 Feed-native

Looks like a story or organic reel, not an ad. Passes the scroll filter.

⚡ Scalable production

Quick hook swaps, new angles without re-shooting. Ideal for volume at $200K/week.

Deliverable 3 — Creative Decisions

Folder 10 + Brand Tone Integration

Although the testimonial angle is alcohol (not weight/sleep), the mechanism is identical: cravings → Kure → control. The authenticity of the delivery outweighs topical precision for performance.

✦ Clean

Minimalist editing: zoom cuts, clean subtitles, no heavy effects. Direct transitions. Visual breathing room throughout.

✦ Science-backed

Subtle text overlay "Clinically-backed hypnotherapy" during the nighttime routine mention. Doesn't force science — integrates it.

✦ Aspirational

The narrative arc goes from problem to transformation: "feeling in control again", "finally feel free". The CTA closes with possibility, not pressure.

Deliverable 3 — Iteration Plan

What If Results Are Unsatisfactory?

W1

Week 1 — Monitor hooks

Compare CTR across the 3 hooks to identify the winning angle. Pause underperformers, scale the winner.

W2

Week 2 — Refresh or restructure

If CTR drops → produce new hooks keeping the same body. If hold rate is low → shorten the video or restructure the middle section.

W3

Week 3 — Deep diagnosis

If ROAS doesn't improve, the problem may be post-click (landing page, pricing, funnel). Coordinate with CRO. In parallel, produce new videos with different creators to avoid fatigue with the same face.

Declining Campaign
Hair Growth Supplement

Question 1 — Diagnosis

The Numbers Tell a Clear Story

WeekCTRCPCROAS
Week 13.2%$0.914.1
Week 22.1%$1.402.8
Week 31.3%$2.101.6

Diagnosis: Creative Fatigue

Creative unchanged, no external factors, and a gradual, consistent decay — the classic pattern of audience saturation. Meta is showing the same ad repeatedly, and each week fewer people engage.

How to confirm: Check frequency in Ads Manager (if >3–4 in week 3 = saturation), breakdown by placement, hook rate week-over-week, and landing page CVR to rule out post-click issues.

Question 2 — Action Plan

Prioritization: Speed → Depth

1

Refresh the hooks

Immediate impact, low cost. Produce 3–5 new hooks for the same base videos. Only swap the first 3–5 seconds. If CTR recovers to 2.5%+ → the hook was the problem. Execution: 1 day.

2

New complete creatives

If hooks don't work, the audience recognizes the whole video. 2–3 new videos with different talent, messaging angle, and visual style. Reset the ad perception. Metric: CTR + hold rate. Execution: 1 week.

3

Audience structure and bidding

If new creatives don't recover ROAS → review whether Meta expanded too broadly (Advantage+), bid cap strategy, landing page and post-click funnel. Metric: ROAS and post-click CVR. Execution: 2+ weeks of testing.
